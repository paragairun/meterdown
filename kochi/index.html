How this Kochi auto fare checker works

Live route via Google Maps

We fetch your exact route with real-time Kochi traffic to get accurate distance and journey time.

Wait time from traffic delay

Traffic delay × 0.9 estimates genuine standstill time — the portion that accrues meter waiting charges.

Official Kerala tariff

₹15.0/km + ₹1.5/min wait + night surcharge, exactly as per May 2022 rates.

±₹5 tolerance band

Meters can differ by a few rupees due to rounding. Only differences beyond ₹5 are flagged as tampering.

Frequently asked questions — Kochi autos

What is the Kochi auto rickshaw fare per km in May 2022?
The rate is ₹15/km as per the Kerala Motor Vehicles Department (May 2022). The minimum fare is ₹30 for the first 1.5 km.
How do I know if my Kochi auto meter is tampered?
Use MeterSahi? — enter your pickup, drop, and the fare charged. If the driver's fare exceeds the calculated correct fare by more than ₹5, tampering is likely. Signs include fares that seem very high for a short distance.
What is the night surcharge for Kochi autos?
A 50% night surcharge applies to all rides between 10 PM – 5 AM.
How do I complain about a tampered auto meter in Kochi?
Call the helpline 91 88 96 11 00 or contact your nearest RTO with the auto's number plate and trip details.
Is MeterSahi? free to use?
Yes, completely free. No registration or app download needed.
