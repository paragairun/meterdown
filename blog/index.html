Protect Yourself from Tampered Autorickshaw Meters in Mumbai

25 June 2025 4 min read Mumbai Consumer rights · Auto fares

Mumbai's bustling streets are filled with autorickshaws — a popular and affordable mode of transport for millions of commuters every day. But a growing number of passengers are falling victim to tampered meters that show inflated fares. Knowing how to spot a tampered meter and what to do about it can save you money and protect your rights as a passenger.

Why Tampered Meters Are a Concern for Mumbai Commuters

Autorickshaws are crucial to Mumbai's daily commute, but meter tampering has become a widespread problem:

  • Overcharging passengers: Manipulated meters tick faster than they should, showing fares much higher than the official tariff rate.
  • Lack of accountability: Drivers can easily deny tampering, leaving passengers with no easy way to prove the discrepancy.
  • Financial loss over time: Frequent commuters who take autos daily may be losing hundreds or even thousands of rupees a month without realising it.
  • Safety concerns: Meter tampering often indicates unregulated, unchecked vehicles operating outside the law.

⚡ How to spot a tampered meter

One of the most reliable tell-tale signs of a tampered auto meter is two blinking dots on the display instead of the usual one. This has been flagged by Mumbai Traffic Police as a known indicator of tampering. Also watch for fares that seem disproportionately high for a short distance — if a 3 km ride costs ₹80+, something is wrong.

Why Mumbai Needs This Tool Now More Than Ever

With crores of auto rides taken every day across Mumbai, even a ₹10–₹20 overcharge per ride adds up quickly. MeterSahi.in puts the power back in the hands of passengers:

  • Save money: Avoid being overcharged on every ride, short or long.
  • Promote fairness: Encourage honest practices among auto drivers — the vast majority of whom are legitimate.
  • Empower passengers: Give commuters the confidence to challenge unfair fares with data, not arguments.
  • Support regulation: Help local authorities identify and act on vehicles with tampered meters.

Tips for Mumbai Commuters to Avoid Tampered Meters

  • Always ask the driver to start the meter at the beginning of your trip — don't negotiate a flat fare.
  • Note the meter reading at the start of the ride, especially if it seems high already.
  • Watch the display — one blinking dot is normal; two blinking dots is a tampering red flag.
  • Use metersahi.in/mumbai/ to cross-check the fare in seconds before you pay.
  • If overcharged, politely note the auto number plate and report via the helpline or RTO.
  • Prefer rides with digital or government-verified meters wherever possible.

How to Complain About a Tampered Meter

If you believe your auto meter has been tampered with, you have several options:

  • Call the Maharashtra Transport Helpline: 1800-233-1922 (toll-free, 24×7).
  • Contact RTO Mumbai Central at 9076201010 or mh01taxicomplaint@gmail.com.
  • Contact RTO Mumbai West at 9920240202 or mh02.autotaxicomplaint@gmail.com.
  • File an online complaint at the Aaple Sarkar portal (aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in).

Keep the auto's number plate and the approximate time of your ride handy before calling.
