How this Mumbai auto fare checker works

Live route via Google Maps

We fetch your exact route with real-time Mumbai traffic to get distance in km and estimated journey time — no guesswork.

Wait time from traffic delay

Google returns free-flow and traffic-adjusted duration. The gap (×0.6) models how long your auto is stationary — which is exactly how meter wait charges accrue.

Official Maharashtra tariff

We apply the exact formula: ₹17.14/km + ₹1.71/min wait + luggage + night surcharge (Feb 2025 rates), rounded just as the meter should.

±₹5 tolerance band

Meters can legitimately differ by a few rupees due to rounding. We only flag differences beyond ₹5 as potential tampering — no false alarms.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Mumbai autorickshaw fare per km in 2025?
The rate is ₹17.14 per km as per the Maharashtra Motor Vehicle Department tariff effective 1 February 2025. The minimum fare is ₹26, covering approximately the first 1.5 km. Before this revision, the minimum was ₹23.
How do I know if my auto meter is tampered?
Use MeterSahi? — enter your pickup and drop and the fare charged. If the driver's fare exceeds our calculated correct fare by more than ₹5, tampering is likely. On the meter itself, two blinking dots (instead of one) is a known sign of tampering flagged by Mumbai Traffic Police.
What is the night surcharge for Mumbai autos?
A 25% night surcharge applies to all rides between 12:00 AM (midnight) and 5:00 AM. This is applied to the total fare including base, waiting, and luggage charges.
How do I complain about a tampered auto meter?
You have several options:
  • Call Maharashtra Transport Helpline: 1800-233-1922 (toll-free, 24×7)
  • RTO Mumbai Central: 9076201010 or mh01taxicomplaint@gmail.com
  • RTO Mumbai West: 9920240202 or mh02.autotaxicomplaint@gmail.com
  • File online at Aaple Sarkar portal (aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in)
Keep the auto's number plate and time of ride handy when filing the complaint.
Is it free to use MeterSahi?
Yes, completely free. No registration, no app download needed. Just open the website, enter your route, and get your fare check instantly.
What is the waiting charge for Mumbai autos?
The waiting charge is ₹1.71 per minute, which accrues whenever the vehicle is stationary — at signals, in traffic jams, or waiting outside a destination.
