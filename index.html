How the fare check works

Live route via Google Maps

We fetch your exact route with real-time traffic to get distance in km and estimated journey duration.

Wait time from traffic delay

Google returns both free-flow and traffic-adjusted duration. The gap (×0.6) models the time your auto is stationary — exactly how meter wait charges accrue.

Official Maharashtra tariff

We apply the exact formula: ₹17.14/km + ₹1.71/min wait + luggage + night surcharge, then round to the nearest rupee, just like the meter should.

±₹5 tolerance band

Meters can legitimately differ by a few rupees due to rounding. We flag only differences beyond ₹5 as potential tampering.
