How this Chennai auto fare checker works

Live route via Google Maps

We fetch your exact route with real-time Chennai traffic to get accurate distance and journey time.

Wait time from traffic delay

Traffic delay × 0.9 estimates genuine standstill time — the portion that accrues meter waiting charges.

Official Tamil Nadu tariff

₹16.0/km + ₹0.7/min wait + night surcharge, exactly as per Current official rates.

±₹5 tolerance band

Meters can differ by a few rupees due to rounding. Only differences beyond ₹5 are flagged as tampering.

Frequently asked questions — Chennai autos

What is the Chennai auto rickshaw fare per km in Current official?
The rate is ₹16/km as per the Tamil Nadu Motor Vehicles Department (Current official). The minimum fare is ₹25 for the first 1.8 km.
How do I know if my Chennai auto meter is tampered?
Use MeterSahi? — enter your pickup, drop, and the fare charged. If the driver's fare exceeds the calculated correct fare by more than ₹5, tampering is likely. Signs include fares that seem very high for a short distance.
What is the night surcharge for Chennai autos?
A 50% night surcharge applies to all rides between 11 PM – 5 AM.
How do I complain about a tampered auto meter in Chennai?
Call the helpline 1800-425-1616 or contact your nearest RTO with the auto's number plate and trip details.
Is MeterSahi? free to use?
Yes, completely free. No registration or app download needed.
